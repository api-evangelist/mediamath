Hispanic Heritage Month in the United States runs from September 15 to October 15 each year. The half-month-to-half-month structure might seem peculiar, but the reason for this is that these dates overlap with the independence celebrations of a number of Hispanic countries (Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Mexico, and Chile).
